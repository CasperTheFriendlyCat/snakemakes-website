About SnakeMakes

Photo of you / your workshop

Hi, I'm the maker behind SnakeMakes. I started 3D printing as a hobby, and it grew from there - friends and fellow Lorcana players kept asking me to print them deck boxes and accessories, and I realised other people might want them too. I also design and print fish tank decorations and accessories for aquarium keepers.

Everything is printed to order in my own space, so please allow a little time for your item to be made before it ships. If you want something custom, get in touch - I'm always happy to design something new.
